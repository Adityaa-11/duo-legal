Play Duo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Play Duo is a local two-player arcade. Matches run on your device.

Data

Play Duo does not require an account and does not upload gameplay. Scores live in memory for the current match only.

Purchases

Apple processes the Remove Ads purchase. Play Duo receives entitlement status from StoreKit. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Analytics and tracking

Version 1.0 does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. House ads shown in the free lobby are local placeholders until a live ad network is wired.

Children

Play Duo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
