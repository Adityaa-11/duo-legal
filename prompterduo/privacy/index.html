Prompter Duo Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Prompter Duo is designed to keep your work on your device.

Data you create

Scripts and prompting preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple’s SwiftData framework. Prompter Duo does not operate an account system or upload scripts to our servers.

Camera, microphone, and photo library

Camera and microphone access are used only when you choose to preview or record a video. Add-only Photos access is used only when you choose to save a finished recording. Prompter Duo does not access your existing photo library.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and subscriptions. Prompter Duo receives entitlement status and transaction identifiers from StoreKit so it can unlock Pro features. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Analytics and tracking

Version 1.0 does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a script removes it from the app’s local data store. Deleting the app removes its locally stored app data, subject to device backups managed by Apple. Videos saved to Photos remain there until you delete them.

Children

Prompter Duo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
