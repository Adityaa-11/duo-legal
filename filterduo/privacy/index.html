Filter Duo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Filter Duo is designed to keep camera frames and photos on your device.

Camera and photo library

Camera access is used for the live filtered preview and still capture. Add-only Photos access is used only when you take a photo. Filter Duo does not access your existing photo library and does not upload camera frames or photos.

Analytics and tracking

Version 1.0 does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Children

Filter Duo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
