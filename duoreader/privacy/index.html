Duo Reader Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Duo Reader is designed to keep your documents private and on your device.

Documents and library data

PDFs you select are copied into Duo Reader’s private application storage so they remain available offline. Titles, collections, favorites, and reading progress are stored locally using Apple’s SwiftData framework. Duo Reader does not upload document files, document text, filenames, or reading activity to our servers.

File providers

The system Files picker lets you choose PDFs from iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, and other installed File Providers. Duo Reader receives access only to files you explicitly select.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional lifetime Pro purchase. Duo Reader receives entitlement status and transaction identifiers through StoreKit. We do not receive payment-card information.

Analytics and tracking

Version 1.0 contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
